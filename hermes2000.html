Hermes 2000

It is the result of the course Graphic Identity at Beckmans. In the course, we were tasked with reviving old companies by creating new graphic identities for them. Hermes Gothicus was printed in 1625 in Strängnäs and published by Bishop Laurentius Paulinus Gothus. The newspaper was 40 pages long and consisted of German news translated into Swedish. The news covered global events, including topics such as the Thirty Years’ War, the plague, and the reform of the Catholic Church. Only one copy of a single edition has been found. In this project the interest was in learning about this historical period and exploring the aesthetic expression used at the time. What made it even more fascinating was that Hermes Gothicus has no official transcription or translation. However, with the help of ChatGPT, I managed to transcribe and translate the news from 1624, which is now 400 years old. The result was a website with the news from Hermes Gothicus, where one can read the news in modern Swedish and in the older Swedish used during the 1500s to 1700s.
