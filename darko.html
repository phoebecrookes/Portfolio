The World According to Daniel Darko

A magazine created for the stylist Daniel Darko Otchere in a collaboration with Beckmans and Juice Studios. The magazine explores how Daniel creates imaginary worlds for the people he styles and making his creations into reality. Made as a group project with Ida Ädling, Ella Farestam and Wilmer Norrby. Together we created the concept, design and the interview. The photographs used in the magazine belongs to Daniel's previous projects.
