Phoebe Crookes

Ordkonst 2023-2024

Ordkonst is the literary committee of Akademiska Föreningen, and its periodical features prose, poetry, essays, criticism, interviews, and visual art. Over the past few years, I have designed three issues for them. In January 2023, the first designed issue, IKON, was released. In May of the same year, the second issue, Budget, was released, and the third issue, Jubileum, celebrating 30 years of Ordkonst, was released in August 2024.
